📘 HOW TO PLAY

Ten simple steps to become a word hunter

Login or register — your score, streak and solved words are saved to your account.
Choose a category that you like.
Choose a difficulty: Easy 8 lives, Medium 6, Hard 4.
Read the clue carefully — it describes the hidden word.
Guess letters using the keyboard or your device keys.
Avoid losing lives: each wrong guess costs a life and 5 points.
Use hints carefully — the first gives an extra clue, the second reveals a letter (−15 points each).
Complete the whole word to win the round.
Earn points: +10 per letter, +50 bonus, multiplied by your difficulty.
Win several words in a row to build your 🔥 streak!

⚙️ ABOUT THE ENGINE

Engine status

Every rule of WORD HUNT — the word database with clues, hints and facts, the per-player no-repeat selection, lives, scoring, streaks and win/lose — lives in the C++ class WordHuntGame (game.h / game.cpp). This page only sends short text commands such as GUESS|C and paints the state block the engine returns.

emcc -std=c++11 game.cpp -O2 -s MODULARIZE=1 \
     -s EXPORTED_FUNCTIONS=_wordhunt_command -o wasm/game.js

Compile once with Emscripten to run the real C++ in the browser. Without the build, the page uses the offline preview, which reads the word database straight out of game.cpp.
